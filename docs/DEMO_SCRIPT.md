# DealMind AI — Official 60-90s Demo Script
### HackWith Hyderabad 3.0 Presentation Guide

> **Target Audience**: Hackathon Judges, Investors, Enterprise Sales Leaders  
> **Duration**: 75–90 seconds  
> **Primary Presenter Role**: Account Executive / Product Lead

---

### [0:00 – 0:12] Hook & The Core Problem
**Visual**: Start on the DealMind AI Dashboard. Point cursor to the active pipeline and Memory Health Widget.  
**Speaker**:  
> *"Sales representatives don't lose enterprise deals because information doesn't exist in their CRM. They lose deals because crucial context is scattered across weeks of conversations. Generic AI assistants forget previous calls the moment a chat window closes. We built DealMind AI to solve this using Hindsight: an AI sales memory copilot that gets smarter with every single customer conversation."*

---

### [0:12 – 0:25] Account Context: Acme Corp
**Visual**: Click on **Acme Corp** ($180,000 Enterprise Deal in Negotiation).  
**Speaker**:  
> *"Here is our primary account, Acme Corp. We have an ongoing $180,000 cloud modernization deal. Notice the Customer Snapshot: we are working with VP of Engineering Sarah Lin, CTO Marcus Vance, and VP of Procurement David Keller. But instead of reading 20 pages of CRM notes before my call, let's look at DealMind's Memory Timeline."*

---

### [0:25 – 0:40] The Star Feature: Memory Timeline
**Visual**: Click on the **Memory Timeline** tab. Scroll through the chronological cards.  
**Speaker**:  
> *"This is DealMind's persistent memory in action. On September 18, Acme mentioned they are evaluating Competitor X. On September 20, CTO Marcus Vance flagged SOC2 security compliance and migration downtime risk. On September 23, Procurement objected to upfront costs. And on September 25, our manufacturing ROI case study received positive feedback. Hindsight extracted and structured every single one of these facts."*

---

### [0:40 – 0:55] AI Deal Briefing ("Prepare Me for My Next Call")
**Visual**: Click **Prepare Me for My Next Call (AI Briefing)**. Show the structured strategy, talking points, and click **"Memories Used (4)"**.  
**Speaker**:  
> *"Before stepping into tomorrow's executive review, I click 'Prepare Me for My Next Call'. Instead of generic sales platitudes, DealMind uses Hindsight Reflection to synthesize a battle-tested strategy: 'Lead with our 4.2x manufacturing ROI because Acme previously praised financial evidence. Present SOC2 Type II documentation to CTO Marcus Vance upfront to eliminate Competitor X, and propose an annual flat fee to resolve Procurement's objection.' Notice the 'Memories Used' citation—every recommendation is 100% grounded in persistent memory."*

---

### [0:55 – 1:12] Save & Learn: The Continuous Learning Loop
**Visual**: Click **"+ Add Touchpoint"**. Select Acme Corp, type note: *"Marcus Vance agreed our zero-downtime blue/green cutover architecture meets board criteria. Requested final SLA terms."* Click **"Save & Learn"**.  
**Speaker**:  
> *"Now watch what happens when I log a new touchpoint. I enter Marcus Vance's feedback and click 'Save & Learn'. Watch the animation: DealMind retains this into Hindsight memory immediately. The timeline updates in real-time and our memory health increments."*

---

### [1:12 – 1:25] Ask DealMind & The Learning Curve Demo
**Visual**: Switch to **Learning Demo** tab. Highlight the **Before vs. After** comparison card.  
**Speaker**:  
> *"Look at this visual comparison: Without memory, generic AI tells us 'Understand customer needs and discuss ROI.' With DealMind and Hindsight, our sales copilot knows the exact stakeholder roles, the exact competitor, and the proven talking points that close the deal."*

---

### [1:25 – 1:30] Closing Tagline
**Visual**: Hover over the logo in the top-left corner.  
**Speaker**:  
> *"That is DealMind AI: Don't just close deals. Remember how. Thank you!"*
