# DealMind AI — Official Hackathon Demo Video Script
### HackWith Hyderabad 3.0 Video Submission Guide

**Video Title**: DealMind AI: Memory-Powered Sales Intelligence Agent Using Hindsight  
**Length**: ~2 minutes (120 seconds)  
**Format**: Screen recording with voiceover and camera picture-in-picture  

---

### Segment 1: Hook & The B2B Problem (0:00 – 0:25)
- **Visual**: AE looking through cluttered CRM notes and blank ChatGPT prompt window.
- **Audio (Voiceover)**:
  > *"Every B2B sales representative knows the feeling: you have an executive call in 15 minutes, but your deal context is buried across weeks of call transcripts, scattered email threads, and forgotten CRM notes. You open ChatGPT or a generic AI assistant, but it knows nothing about your customer. It starts from zero every single time. Deals stall, objections get re-asked, and customers get frustrated."*

### Segment 2: Introducing DealMind AI (0:25 – 0:45)
- **Visual**: Screen transition to DealMind AI dashboard. Highlighting the clean SaaS UI, Active Deals, and Memory Health Widget.
- **Audio (Voiceover)**:
  > *"Meet DealMind AI: your sales memory that gets smarter with every conversation. Built for HackWith Hyderabad 3.0, DealMind is powered by Hindsight—a semantic memory engine with persistent recall and reflection. DealMind doesn't just chat; it retains customer objections, learns stakeholder preferences, tracks competitor mentions, and synthesizes increasingly personalized deal strategies over time."*

### Segment 3: How Hindsight Works (0:45 – 1:05)
- **Visual**: Diagram showing Hindsight Retain, Recall, and Reflect operations, with actual code snippets and the Hindsight Status badge.
- **Audio (Voiceover)**:
  > *"Under the hood, DealMind integrates Hindsight's core triad:
  > 1. RETAIN extracts and stores customer beliefs, pricing pushback, and competitor evaluations into a persistent memory bank.
  > 2. RECALL instantly retrieves relevant historical facts with grounded citations.
  > 3. REFLECT reasons across multiple past interactions to formulate winning meeting strategies."*

### Segment 4: Live Demonstration: Acme Corp Journey (1:05 – 1:40)
- **Visual**:
  1. Open Acme Corp. Show the Customer Snapshot and 5 chronological memory cards on the **Memory Timeline**.
  2. Click **'Prepare Me for My Next Call'** to reveal the AI Deal Briefing. Click **'Memories Used (4)'** to show exact grounded facts.
  3. Open **'Save & Learn'** modal, log an interaction, and watch the real-time retention animation.
  4. Navigate to **Learning Demo** and show the side-by-side **Before vs. After Memory** card.
- **Audio (Voiceover)**:
  > *"Watch this in action with Acme Corp. On the Memory Timeline, we see the complete deal evolution: an initial objection to migration downtime from Sarah Lin, a strict SOC2 compliance requirement from CTO Marcus Vance, and an enthusiastic response to our manufacturing ROI case study.
  > When we generate the Deal Briefing, DealMind doesn't give us generic advice. It tells us to lead with the 4.2x ROI payback model and present SOC2 enclave proofs upfront to eliminate Competitor X. 
  > And whenever we log a new touchpoint via 'Save & Learn', Hindsight retains the insight in real-time."*

### Segment 5: Business Impact & Closing (1:40 – 2:00)
- **Visual**: Dashboard overview showing $1M+ pipeline under memory intelligence.
- **Audio (Voiceover)**:
  > *"By cutting pre-call research from 45 minutes down to 30 seconds and giving every sales rep the cumulative memory of their best interactions, DealMind transforms sales execution.
  > Built with FastAPI, React, SQLite, and Hindsight.
  > DealMind AI: Don't just close deals. Remember how.
  > Thank you, HackWith Hyderabad 3.0!"*
