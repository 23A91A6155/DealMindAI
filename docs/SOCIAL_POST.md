### LinkedIn / Professional Announcement

🚀 Excited to unveil **DealMind AI** — our project built for **HackWith Hyderabad 3.0**!

In enterprise sales, deals rarely close in one call. They unfold across months of stakeholder discussions, technical reviews, and shifting objections. 

Yet sales reps repeatedly face the same challenge:
❌ Wasting hours re-reading messy CRM notes  
❌ Forgetting what previous objections were raised  
❌ Using generic AI assistants that suffer from context amnesia and forget the deal the moment the window closes  

We built **DealMind AI** to solve this using **Hindsight**, a persistent semantic memory engine. 

Instead of treating AI like a stateless chatbot, DealMind turns it into a deal memory that gets smarter with every conversation:

🧠 **Retain**: Automatically captures customer objections, competitor evaluations, and stakeholder preferences from every touchpoint.  
🔍 **Recall**: Instantly retrieves specific deal context and past outcomes with visible "Memories Used" citations.  
🎯 **Reflect**: Synthesizes actionable, battle-tested meeting strategies tailored to what actually worked in past interactions ("Prepare Me for My Next Call").  

In our live 60-second learning demo with Acme Corp, you can see the clear before-and-after:
- **Without Memory**: Generic sales advice ("Understand customer needs and explain product features").
- **With Hindsight**: A personalized gameplan that leads with our 4.2x ROI payback model, addresses CTO Marcus Vance's SOC2 compliance concerns upfront, and neutralizes Competitor X.
